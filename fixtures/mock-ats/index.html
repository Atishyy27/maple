Mock application boards

Two self-contained fixtures for testing a label-resolving autofill engine. Nothing here hardcodes a selector; every control is addressed by its human label.

  • Northwind Logistics Three-step wizard. Button combobox over a role=listbox, a field that only exists after a specific option is chosen, digits-only phone validation, and repeating Work Experience / Education rows with generated id prefixes.
  • Vertex Analytics Single page. Clean labels, a name-variant leak trap, proximity-only captions with no for= or aria, aria radios for experience brackets, an unanswerable "excluding internships" field, and a consent box that must stay unticked.

Serve with npm run serve, then point the engine at http://localhost:3939/northwind.html.
